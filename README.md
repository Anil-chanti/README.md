# Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This dashboard analyzes sales and revenue data to generate business insights.

## Features
- Total Sales KPI
- Total Revenue KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Revenue Trend Analysis
- Top Performing Products
- Sales by Category
- Interactive Filters and Slicers

## Visualizations
- Revenue Trend Line Chart
- Sales by Product Bar Chart
- Category-wise Sales Pie Chart
- Regional Sales Analysis
- Monthly Sales vs Revenue Comparison

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI

## Expected Outcome
The dashboard helps track KPIs, monitor sales performance, and support data-driven business decisions.
